Line-Following & Obstacle-Avoidance Robot

Some robots follow instructions. Others follow a line and figure things out when something gets in the way.

Two IR sensors underneath track a black line and continuously correct the robot's heading — both on the line means forward, one off means turn. An ultrasonic sensor up front adds a second layer: when something appears within range, the robot abandons the line, backs up, steers around the obstacle, then hunts for the line again before carrying on.

Popsicle-Stick Robot Arm

Three servo motors, three potentiometers, and a bunch of popsicle sticks walk into a workshop… and leave as a robotic arm.

Each potentiometer drives one degree of freedom — up/down, left/right, wrist rotation. The servos are positioned to balance range against stability, so it lifts small objects without straining the motors.

Smart Trash Can

A hands-free bin that opens when you walk up to it. An ultrasonic sensor watches for distance and a servo lifts the lid through a reinforced popsicle-stick arm. The goal was to make throwing something out more hygienic and slightly cooler than a normal trash can.

Machined Aluminium Pen

This isn't just a pen — it's my pen. Sketches first, then an orthographic drawing at 1:2 scale (5″ × 0.5″). Turned the tip from a cylindrical blank on the metal lathe, drilled a centred bore for the cartridge, cut grooves and knurling, then matched the body so the two align perfectly. Threaded both halves with a tap-and-die set so they screw together.
